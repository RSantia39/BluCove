About Us

Welcome to BluCove, a private pool resort created for those who want more than just a vacation. Here, every detail is designed to help you slow down and enjoy life at your own pace—whether it’s lounging by your private pool, savoring a quiet meal under the palms, or simply breathing in the calm that surrounds you. We’re here to give you comfort, privacy, and unforgettable moments of peace, so that when you leave, you carry the feeling of serenity with you.
